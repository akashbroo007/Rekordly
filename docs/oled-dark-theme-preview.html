Rekordly · Design system

OLED true-black dark theme

Primary background is now true #000000 for OLED. Hierarchy is carried by a four-step neutral ladder — never by hue — plus a 9% white hairline border. Text tops out at off-white #EBEBEE (never pure white) to avoid halation. Accent stays brand-purple, nudged one luminance step so it keeps AA contrast on true black. Status colors unchanged.

Low light: #000 fills mean no gray rectangle glow — only active content emits light, and the muted ramp (#83838D) stays ≥ 4.6:1 even at 45% brightness. Bright/blown-out rooms: panels at #0A0A0A can visually merge in reflections; the 9% hairline border and card fill at #121212 keep card edges readable.
